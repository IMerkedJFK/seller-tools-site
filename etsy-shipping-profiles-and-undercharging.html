Etsy shipping profiles: what an undercharged label really costs

A shipping profile tells Etsy what to charge buyers for postage. If the amount you charge is lower than the label you buy, the gap comes straight out of profit. Reviewed October 2026.

How a profile works

A shipping profile is a saved set of shipping settings that you attach to listings. It holds your processing time, where you ship from and where you ship to. For the price, Etsy lets you choose between a fixed amount that you set, a calculated price based on the package weight and size you enter, or free shipping. Menus and options change over time, so check the shipping settings in your shop manager for what is offered today.

Fixed prices are simple but easy to get wrong when an item is heavier than you assumed. Calculated shipping follows the carrier rate more closely, but only if the weight and dimensions you enter include the box and padding.

Example: a $2.50 shortfall

An item sells for $25.00 and costs you $9.00 to make. The label costs you $7.50. Compare charging $5.00 for shipping with charging the full $7.50.

Shipping chargedBuyer paysTotal feesYou keep
$5.00$30.00$3.30$10.20
$7.50$32.50$3.54$12.46

Fees at $30.00: transaction fee 6.5% is $1.95, processing is 3% ($0.90) plus $0.25, and the listing fee is $0.20, which makes $3.30. You keep $30.00 minus $3.30, minus $9.00 for the item, minus $7.50 for the label, which is $10.20.

At $32.50 the transaction fee is $2.11, processing is $0.98 plus $0.25, and listing is $0.20, so fees round to $3.54. You keep $12.46.

The shortfall of $2.50 costs you $2.26 in profit, not the full $2.50, because charging shipping also raises fees by about $0.24. Shipping is part of the amount the fees are figured on, as covered in the transaction fee guide.

A quick audit

Weigh three of your best selling items in their finished packaging. Look up the real label price to a near zone and a far zone. If the cheapest price you charge is below the label to the far zone, decide whether to raise it, use calculated shipping, or fold postage into the item price. Folding it in is how free shipping works, covered in pricing free shipping on Etsy. Etsy changes fees from time to time, so confirm current rates at etsy.com/legal/fees.
