How much does it cost to sell on Etsy?

For a US seller the fees that apply to a typical sale are the $0.20 listing fee, a 6.5 percent transaction fee, payment processing of 3 percent plus $0.25, and, only on orders that come from an Etsy ad, an Offsite Ads fee of 15 or 12 percent. Here is one sale worked through from start to finish. Reviewed October 2026.

The example sale

A $25 item, $5 shipping charged to the buyer, 8 percent sales tax collected by Etsy, and $8 of your own costs for materials and packaging. The buyer pays $32.40.

FeeHow it is worked outAmount
Listing feeFlat$0.20
Transaction fee6.5% of $30.00 (item plus shipping, not tax)$1.95
Payment processing3% of $32.40 plus $0.25 (includes shipping and tax)$1.22
Total fees$3.37

You keep $18.63 after fees and your $8.00 of costs, a margin of about 62.1 percent of the $30.00 you charged for item and shipping.

The same sale from an Offsite Ad

If the buyer came through an ad Etsy ran for you, add the Offsite Ads fee. At the 15 percent rate it is 15 percent of $30.00, which is $4.50. Total fees become $7.87 and you keep $14.13, a margin of about 47.1 percent. That is why it is worth pricing for the ad case if you expect ad orders. Shops with $10,000 or more in sales over the prior 365 days pay 12 percent and cannot opt out. Etsy caps the ad fee at $100 per order.

What this does not include

This only covers the fees above. It leaves out currency conversion (2.5 percent when your listing currency differs from your payment account currency), optional purchases such as Etsy Ads or shipping labels, refunds, and your income taxes.

How to use this

Run your own numbers rather than copying these. Price your item so that after every fee and every cost you still reach the margin you want. The break even price formula shows how.
