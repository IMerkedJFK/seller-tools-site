Etsy fees on cheap items

Etsy's percentage fees are the same on every sale, but its flat fees are not a percentage of anything. The $0.20 listing fee and the $0.25 processing charge come to $0.45 on every order, and on a low priced item that is a large share. Reviewed October 2026.

These figures assume US sellers, no shipping charged, no sales tax and no ads. They include the 6.5 percent transaction fee and processing of 3 percent plus $0.25.

What to do about it

Sell bundles or sets so one order carries more value. Raise the price rather than hoping for volume. Check your cheapest items first with the break even formula, because they are the most likely to lose money. If a cheap item gets an Offsite Ads sale, add 15 or 12 percent on top.
