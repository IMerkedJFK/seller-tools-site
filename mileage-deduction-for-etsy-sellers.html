Mileage deduction for Etsy sellers

Trips for your business, such as a drive to the post office to ship orders, to buy supplies or to a craft fair, may be deductible. The IRS standard mileage rate for business use in 2026 is 72.5 cents per mile. Reviewed October 2026. Confirm the current rate at irs.gov.

Example

120 business miles in a month at 72.5 cents is $87.00 of mileage deduction. Over a year of regular trips this adds up, but only trips for the business count, and commuting does not.

What to log

Keep a record at the time of each trip: the date, the miles, where you went and why. A simple log is far easier than reconstructing a year later. The IRS can ask for records to support what you claim.

Rules vary by situation

Whether you can use the standard rate depends on your facts, for example how you use the vehicle. Ask a qualified tax professional, or read the IRS guidance on business use of a car.
