Etsy refunds and seller fees

Refunding an order is part of selling, and it helps to know which fees come back and which do not. Reviewed October 2026, based on Etsy's help article on issuing refunds.

What Etsy says comes back

When you refund through Shop Manager, Etsy states that the transaction and processing fees for the refunded transactions are automatically refunded to your Payment account. The listing fee is different. Etsy says it is credited back only if you also cancel the transaction. A refund on its own leaves the $0.20 listing fee spent.

Two other details from Etsy: refunds through Etsy Payments are available up to 180 days after the payment is processed, and the money for a refund comes out of your Payment account balance. If that balance is too low, the card on file is charged for the rest. Refunding outside Etsy, for example by another payment service after the 180 days, means Etsy cannot return your fees.

Worked example

A $30.00 item sells with free shipping, and you pay $5.00 for the shipping label. The original fees are:

FeeMathAmount
Transaction fee6.5% of $30.00$1.95
Processing fee3% of $30.00 plus $0.25$1.15
Listing feeflat$0.20
Total$3.30

The buyer asks for a full refund. If you refund through Shop Manager without canceling the transaction, $1.95 and $1.15 come back, which is $3.10. You are still out the $0.20 listing fee and the $5.00 label, so the order costs you $5.20 even if the item returns in resalable condition. If you cancel the transaction before shipping, the listing fee is credited too, and you lose nothing on fees.

Partial refunds

Partial refunds are common for damage or delays. Etsy says fees for the refunded transactions are returned, but check the order in Shop Manager to see the exact fee amounts adjusted on your statement, because the details of how a partial amount is treated can change.

What to do with this

Treat refunds as a normal cost of selling. Many sellers add a small allowance to prices to cover the lost label money and the occasional listing fee that stays spent. Record every refund with its date and order number so your income and expenses match your statements at tax time. Fees change, so confirm current rules at etsy.com/legal/fees.
