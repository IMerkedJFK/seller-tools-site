Quarterly estimated taxes for Etsy sellers (US)

This is general information, not tax advice. Confirm dates and rules with the IRS or a qualified professional.

Why quarterly

Nobody withholds tax from your shop income, so the IRS expects you to pay during the year if you expect to owe a meaningful amount. Many sellers pay four times a year.

Typical due dates

April 15, June 15, September 15 and January 15 of the following year. When a date falls on a weekend or holiday it moves, so check the current calendar.

A simple way to plan

Estimate your net profit for the year, work out self employment tax and income tax on it, and divide by four. Or set aside a fixed share of each month's profit in a separate account.

Safe harbor

The IRS generally does not charge an underpayment penalty if you pay enough during the year, commonly 90 percent of this year's tax or 100 percent of last year's tax (110 percent for higher incomes). Ask a professional which applies to you.
