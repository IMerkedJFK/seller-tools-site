Self employment tax for Etsy sellers (US)

This is general information, not tax advice. Rules and rates change, so confirm with the IRS or a qualified professional.

The basics

If you sell on Etsy as an individual, the IRS generally treats your shop as a business. You report income and expenses on Schedule C. Besides income tax, you may owe self employment tax, which covers Social Security and Medicare.

How it is calculated

Self employment tax is 15.3 percent, applied to 92.35 percent of your net profit. You generally owe it if your net profit is $400 or more. Half of it can be deducted when working out your income tax.

A simple example

With a $1,339 net profit, 92.35 percent is $1,237, and 15.3 percent of that is about $189 of self employment tax.

What this means for you

Track every sale and every business expense through the year. Net profit, not sales, is what the tax is based on. Setting aside a share of each month's profit makes tax time much less painful.
