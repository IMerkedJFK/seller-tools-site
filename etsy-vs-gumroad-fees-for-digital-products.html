Etsy vs Gumroad fees for digital products

Both sites let you sell a digital download, and both take a cut. Here is what you keep on three prices using the fees each site publishes. Reviewed October 2026.

The fees used

Etsy (US, no ads, no sales tax, no shipping): $0.20 listing fee, 6.5 percent transaction fee, 3 percent plus $0.25 payment processing. Gumroad: its pricing page lists 10 percent plus $0.50 per sale for direct sales (your own link or profile) and 30 percent for sales that come through Gumroad Discover. The Gumroad page does not mention separate payment processing charges, so check gumroad.com/pricing for your own situation.

How to read it

On these three prices Etsy and a Gumroad direct sale keep you within a few cents of each other. The difference is not the fee, it is who brings the buyer. Etsy has its own search traffic but charges extra (15 or 12 percent) when an ad brings the buyer. A Gumroad direct sale needs you to bring the traffic yourself, for example from a website or social posts. A Discover sale costs the most.

Selling in both places

Many sellers list the same file on both. Price each channel with its own fees in mind. Use the Etsy fee calculator for the Etsy side, and see Etsy fees for digital downloads for more on that listing type.
