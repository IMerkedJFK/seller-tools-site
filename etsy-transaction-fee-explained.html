Etsy transaction fee explained

Etsy charges a transaction fee of 6.5 percent on each sale. It applies to the item price plus any shipping you charge the buyer. It does not apply to sales tax. Reviewed October 2026.

Example

A $30 item with $4 shipping charged to the buyer. The transaction fee is 6.5 percent of $34.00, which is $2.21.

Does free shipping change it?

If you raise the item price to $34 and offer free shipping, the buyer still pays $34.00 in total and the transaction fee is the same $2.21. The fee follows the total of item and shipping, so moving cost between the two does not avoid it. What changes the number is the total you charge.

Where it fits in your price

The transaction fee is the largest percentage on a normal sale, bigger than the 3 percent processing rate. Together with processing, the percentage fees are 9.5 percent of what the buyer pays you for item and shipping, before the flat fees and any ad fee.
