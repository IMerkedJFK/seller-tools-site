Pricing bundles on Etsy

A bundle sells several items in one order, usually at a small discount. The discount feels expensive, but part of it comes back to you because you pay fewer fixed fees. Reviewed October 2026.

Why bundles cost you less than they seem

Etsy charges a $0.20 listing fee each time a listing sells and renews, and a $0.25 payment processing charge on each order. Both are flat amounts. Sell three items in one order and you pay each of them once instead of three times. The percentage fees, 6.5% transaction fee and 3% processing, depend only on the amount the buyer pays. Shipping is left out of the example below to keep the math clear.

Example

Each item sells for $10.00 and costs you $4.00 to make. A buyer could buy three separately, or you could offer a three item bundle for $26.00, which is $4.00 off.

SetupBuyer paysTotal feesYour costsYou keep
Three separate orders$30.00$4.20$12.00$13.80
One bundle order$26.00$2.92$12.00$11.08

Separate orders: each order has fees of $0.65 plus $0.55 plus $0.20, which is $1.40, and three of them make $4.20. Bundle: $1.69 transaction fee, $1.03 processing ($0.78 plus $0.25) and $0.20 listing, which is $2.92.

The bundle gives away $4.00 of price but fees fall by $1.28, so your profit drops by only $2.72. That gap, $13.80 minus $11.08, is the real price of the discount.

How to set your bundle price

Start from your total cost for every item in the bundle, including packaging and the label if it ships in one parcel. Add the flat fees once, then divide by what is left after the percentage fees, as in the break even formula. That gives a floor. Any bundle price above it makes money, and you choose how much of the extra to hand back as a discount.

Two cautions. A bundle only saves postage if the items really ship together, so weigh and price the parcel honestly. And if you list the bundle as its own listing, remember that every sale renews it, so the listing fee applies again. Etsy changes fees from time to time, so confirm current rates at etsy.com/legal/fees.
