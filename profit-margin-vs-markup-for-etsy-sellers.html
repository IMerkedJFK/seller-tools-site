Profit margin vs markup

Markup is profit divided by cost. Margin is profit divided by price. They are different numbers for the same sale, and mixing them up is a common reason sellers underprice. This page uses simple math before Etsy fees.

The difference

CostMarkupPriceMargin
$10.0025%$12.5020.0%
$10.0050%$15.0033.3%
$10.00100%$20.0050.0%
$10.00150%$25.0060.0%

A 50 percent markup on a $10.00 cost gives a $15.00 price and a margin of one third, not 50 percent. To get a 50 percent margin you need a 100 percent markup.

Now add Etsy fees

Fees come out of the price, so they cut your margin further. Price from the margin you want, then solve for the price that survives the fees. The break even and target margin formulas do that. For example, a $4 cost and a 40 percent margin after fees means a buyer total of $8.81, which is a markup of about 120 percent on cost.
