Etsy listing fee explained

Every Etsy listing costs $0.20 to publish. That sounds small, but it is charged more than once, and for cheap items it matters.

When you pay it

You pay $0.20 when a listing is first published. You pay it again each time the item sells, because a sale renews the listing, and again if a listing expires and you renew it. A listing with a quantity of five that sells all five times can cost you the fee five times over.

Why it matters on cheap items

On a $5 item, the $0.20 listing fee plus the $0.25 payment processing charge is $0.45 before any percentages are added. That is 9 percent of the price in flat fees alone. On a $50 item the same $0.45 is under 1 percent.

How to build it into your price

Treat $0.20 as a cost on every sale. In the pricing formula, add it to the other flat amounts: 0.20 plus 0.25 equals 0.45 that you add to your own costs before you divide by what is left after the percentage fees.

Always confirm the current amount at etsy.com/legal/fees, since Etsy updates fees from time to time.
