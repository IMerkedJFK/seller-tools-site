Etsy fees for digital downloads

Digital products have no shipping and no materials, so more of the sale is profit. The fees are the same as for physical goods, and the flat parts bite hardest at low prices.

What you keep

PriceFeesYou keep
$5$0.93$4.07
$8$1.21$6.79
$12$1.59$10.41
$25$2.83$22.18

These assume no ads and no currency conversion. The $8 row works out as 0.20 plus 6.5 percent of 8 plus 3 percent of 8 plus 0.25.

Tips

Bundle related files so the flat fees are spread over a bigger sale. Check that your price still leaves a profit when a sale comes from an Offsite Ad. And remember that your time creating the file is a real cost, even though each sale costs you nothing to fulfil.
