Etsy break even price formula

You can work out the lowest price that covers your costs and Etsy's fees, and the price that reaches a margin you choose, with two short formulas. This version is for US sellers with no ads, no currency conversion and no sales tax. Reviewed October 2026.

The formulas

Let R be what the buyer pays you for item and shipping together, and C be your own cost including materials, packaging and any shipping label you pay for.

Etsy fees are 0.45 plus 0.095 times R. The 0.45 is the $0.20 listing fee plus the $0.25 processing fee. The 0.095 is the 6.5 percent transaction fee plus the 3 percent processing rate.

Break even R = (0.45 + C) divided by 0.905.

R for a target margin M, written as a decimal (40 percent is 0.40) = (0.45 + C) divided by (0.905 minus M).

Example

Your cost is $4 and you want a 40 percent margin. R = 4.45 divided by 0.505 = $8.81. Fees are about $1.29 and profit is about $3.52, which is about 40 percent of $8.81. Etsy rounds each fee to the cent, so your own check may differ by a cent.

When the shortcut does not hold

If you expect Offsite Ads orders, replace 0.905 with 0.755 (15 percent rate) or 0.785 (12 percent rate), but only while the ad fee stays under Etsy's $100 cap. Sales tax and currency conversion add small amounts the formulas leave out. The free calculator handles all of them, including the cap.
