Pricing free shipping on Etsy

Free shipping does not remove the shipping cost. It moves it into your item price. Reviewed October 2026.

Example

Your cost for an order is $9.00, including the shipping label you pay for. You could charge $15.00 for the item and $5.00 shipping, or $20.00 with free shipping. The buyer pays $20.00 either way.

SetupBuyer paysTotal feesYou keep
$15.00 item plus $5.00 shipping$20.00$2.35$8.65
$20.00 item, free shipping$20.00$2.35$8.65

The transaction fee applies to item plus shipping, so both setups produce the same fees. The fees follow the total.

The mistake to avoid

If you offer free shipping but leave the item at $16.50, you give up the shipping you used to charge. Fees fall to $2.02 but you keep only $5.48 instead of $8.65. Work out the total you need first, using the target margin formula, then decide how to split it.

Free shipping and sales tax

Sales tax is charged on what the buyer pays and affects processing slightly. See the processing fee guide.
