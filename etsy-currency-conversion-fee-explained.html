Etsy currency conversion fee explained

Etsy charges a currency conversion fee of 2.5 percent when you list in a currency that is different from the currency of your payment account. Reviewed October 2026. A US seller who lists in US dollars and is paid into a US dollar account does not pay it.

Examples

Item priceCurrency conversion fee (2.5%)Total fees with conversion
$20.00$0.50$2.85
$50.00$1.25$6.45

The totals include the $0.20 listing fee, the 6.5 percent transaction fee, and payment processing of 3 percent plus $0.25. Our calculator applies the 2.5 percent to the item price plus shipping you charge. Etsy describes it as a percentage of the sale amount, so check an actual order in your own payment account if the exact base matters to you.

What it means for pricing

The fee adds 2.5 points to your percentage fees, so your break even price rises. If you sell across currencies, include it when you work out your target price. In our free calculator tick the currency conversion box to include it.
