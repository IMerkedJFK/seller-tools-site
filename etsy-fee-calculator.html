Free Etsy fee calculator (US sellers)

Built for US sellers using US dollars and Etsy Payments with a US bank account. Other countries have different fees, so this will not be accurate for them. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is saved or sent. Refunds and returns are not modelled, so negative numbers are rejected.

How it works

Fees follow Etsy's published US rules, last reviewed October 2026 against etsy.com/legal/fees and the Etsy Payments policy. Listing fee $0.20. Transaction fee 6.5% of item price plus shipping you charge (sales tax is excluded). Payment processing 3% plus $0.25 on the full amount the buyer pays, including shipping and sales tax. Offsite Ads, only on orders that came through an ad: 15% for shops under $10,000 in sales over the prior 365 days, 12% at $10,000 or more, with a $100 cap on the fee for any single order. Currency conversion 2.5% on the sale amount when you list in a currency other than your payment account currency.

Assumptions to know

Sales tax is assumed to apply to item price plus shipping at the rate you enter, which varies by location. The currency conversion fee is applied to item price plus shipping. Etsy decides eligibility for the lower Offsite Ads rate and whether you can opt out, so check your Shop Manager. Break even and target prices solve for the item price with your shipping charge held fixed. Prices are rounded to the cent line by line, as Etsy charges each fee separately. Etsy changes fees from time to time, so confirm current rates before relying on a result.
