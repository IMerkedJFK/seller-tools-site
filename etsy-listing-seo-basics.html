Etsy listing SEO basics: titles, tags and photos

Etsy search tries to match what a shopper types to what your listing says, then to rank matches by things like relevance and how shoppers respond. Nobody outside Etsy knows the exact formula, so treat any guide, including this one, as good habits rather than guarantees. Reviewed November 2026. Etsy changes its tools, so confirm current limits in Etsy's Seller Handbook.

Write the title for people first

Etsy has allowed titles up to 140 characters. Longer is not automatically better. Put the clearest description of the item first, in the words a buyer would actually type, such as "Soy candle, lavender, 8 oz glass jar". Skip repeating the same word many times, since it makes the title hard to read and rarely helps.

Use all of your tags, with phrases

Etsy has allowed up to 13 tags per listing, each up to 20 characters. Tags work best as short phrases a shopper might search, not single words. Count carefully, because spaces count. "handmade soy candle" is 8 + 1 + 3 + 1 + 6 = 19 characters, so it fits. "lavender candle gift" is 8 + 1 + 6 + 1 + 4 = 20 characters, which just fits. Add one more word and it would not.

Avoid repeating the exact same phrase in several tags. Use each tag to cover a different angle, such as the item type, the occasion, the recipient or the style.

Fill in attributes and categories

Choose the most specific category and complete the attribute fields, such as color, occasion and material. These help your listing show up when shoppers filter their results, and they are free to fill in.

Photos matter as much as words

Shoppers decide quickly from the thumbnail. Use a clear first photo on a plain background, good natural light, and the item filling most of the frame. Add photos that show scale, detail and the item in use. Etsy has allowed up to 20 photos per listing, but a handful of strong ones beats many weak ones.

Do not forget the price side

More views only help if each sale leaves you with profit. Before you chase traffic, run your price through the free Etsy fee calculator to see what remains after fees. The Etsy Profit Calculator is a paid spreadsheet option if you want to compare several products at once. For the pricing method, see the break even price formula.

A simple routine

Pick one listing a week. Rewrite the title in plain words, fill every tag with a distinct phrase, complete the attributes, and replace your weakest photo. Then wait a few weeks before judging, since search results shift slowly and many things affect sales.

This is general information, not a promise of search ranking.
