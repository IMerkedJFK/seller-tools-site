Etsy payment processing fee explained

For US sellers using Etsy Payments with a US bank account, Etsy charges 3 percent plus $0.25 on each order. The part that surprises people is what it is charged on: the whole amount the buyer pays, which includes shipping and any sales tax. Reviewed October 2026.

Three examples

SaleBuyer paysProcessing fee
$12 item, no shipping, no tax$12.00$0.61
$12 item, no shipping, 8% sales tax$12.96$0.64
$40 item, $6 shipping, 7% sales tax$49.22$1.73

In the first row, 3 percent of $12.00 is $0.36, plus $0.25, gives $0.61. In the third row, 3 percent of $49.22 is $1.48, plus $0.25, gives $1.73 after rounding to the cent.

Why the flat $0.25 matters

The $0.25 is the same on a $5 order and a $500 order, so it takes the biggest share of cheap items. Add the $0.20 listing fee and the flat part of your fees is $0.45 on every sale.

Sales tax and your price

Sales tax that Etsy collects from the buyer is passed on to the tax authority, but you still pay processing on it. The effect is small, about 3 percent of the tax, but it is real. Our fee calculator has a sales tax field so it shows up in your result.

Other fees on the same order

The transaction fee and the listing fee are separate. If you list in a currency different from your payment account, a 2.5 percent currency conversion fee also applies. Confirm current amounts at etsy.com/legal/fees.
