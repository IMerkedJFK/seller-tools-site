Home office deduction for Etsy sellers

If you run your shop from part of your home, you may be able to deduct a home office. The IRS offers a simplified method: $5 per square foot of qualifying space, for up to 300 square feet, so the most you can claim this way is $1,500 a year. Reviewed November 2026. Confirm the details at irs.gov.

Who may qualify

The space generally must be used regularly and exclusively for your business, and it must be your principal place of business. A desk in the living room that your family also uses usually does not meet the exclusive use test. A spare room used only for crafting, photographing and packing orders is the kind of space people ask about. Your facts matter, so check the IRS rules or ask a tax professional.

Worked example

Say your workroom measures 10 feet by 12 feet. That is 120 square feet. At $5 per square foot the deduction is 120 x $5 = $600 for the year. A larger space is capped: 300 square feet x $5 = $1,500, and anything above 300 square feet adds nothing under this method.

Simplified method vs regular method

The regular method uses the share of your home the office takes up, applied to actual costs such as rent, utilities and insurance. If your home is 1,500 square feet, the 120 square foot room is 8 percent (120 / 1,500 = 0.08). With $15,000 of yearly housing costs, 8 percent is $1,200. The regular method needs more records but can produce a bigger number. The simplified method is quicker and needs fewer records. Compare both for your own numbers before you file.

Does it lower your tax?

For a sole proprietor, a deduction reduces net profit, which can lower both income tax and self employment tax. A $600 deduction at the 15.3 percent self employment tax rate is about $92 of that tax alone (600 x 0.153 = $91.80), before any income tax effect. Limits can apply if your business has little profit, so confirm with the IRS.

Keep simple records

Write down the room measurements, a note on how the space is used, and the dates you used it for business. Pair this with your fees and shipping costs, which the free Etsy fee calculator helps you estimate per sale.
