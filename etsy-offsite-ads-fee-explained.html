Etsy Offsite Ads fee explained

Offsite Ads are ads Etsy runs for your listings on other sites. You only pay when a buyer clicks an ad and then buys from you within the attribution window Etsy sets.

The rates

Shops that earned under $10,000 in the past year pay 15 percent of the order total on ad orders and cannot opt out. Shops above that pay 12 percent and can opt out. Etsy also caps the fee on any single order, so check the current cap in its policy pages.

What it does to profit

On a $25 item with no shipping, normal Etsy fees come to $2.83. If the buyer arrived through an Offsite Ad, add 15 percent, which is $3.75. Fees become $6.58, about 26 percent of the price, and you keep $18.43 before your own costs.

How to price for it

Not every order will come from an ad, so you do not need to price everything for the worst case. A simple approach is to price so that you still make a profit, even a small one, on an ad order. In the pricing formulas, replace the 0.905 figure with 0.755 to see that worst case.
