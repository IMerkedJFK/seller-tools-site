Etsy Offsite Ads fee explained

Offsite Ads are ads Etsy runs for your listings on other sites. You only pay when a buyer clicks an ad and then buys from you within the attribution window Etsy sets.

The rates

Per Etsy's fee policy, shops with less than $10,000 in sales over the prior 365 days pay 15 percent on ad orders, and can opt out of Offsite Ads. Shops with $10,000 or more over the prior 365 days pay the lower 12 percent, and participation is required: if you opted out before reaching the $10,000 threshold, you will be required to participate once you reach it. The fee is calculated on the item price plus shipping and gift wrapping you charge, and Etsy caps the Offsite Ads fee at $100 on any single order. Etsy decides eligibility, so confirm your status in Shop Manager.

What it does to profit

On a $25 item with no shipping, normal Etsy fees come to $2.83. If the buyer arrived through an Offsite Ad, add 15 percent, which is $3.75. Fees become $6.58, about 26 percent of the price, and you keep $18.42 before your own costs. Because of the $100 cap, the ad fee stops growing on large orders: a $1,000 item with a 15 percent ad pays $100, not $150, so total fees on that order are $195.45 rather than $245.45 (US assumptions, no sales tax or currency conversion).

How to price for it

Not every order will come from an ad, so you do not need to price everything for the worst case. A simple approach is to price so that you still make a profit, even a small one, on an ad order. In the pricing formulas, replace the 0.905 figure with 0.755 to see that worst case, or 0.785 at the 12 percent rate. This shortcut only holds while the ad fee is under the $100 cap, so for orders above about $667 at 15 percent (or $833 at 12 percent) use the fee calculator, which applies the cap.
