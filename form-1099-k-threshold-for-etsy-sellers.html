Form 1099 K threshold for Etsy sellers

Form 1099 K is a form that payment platforms send to sellers and to the IRS. Reviewed October 2026. The IRS says the federal reporting threshold went back to more than $20,000 in payments and more than 200 transactions in a year, after a law passed in 2025 reversed the planned lower threshold. A platform only files the form for you if you cross both numbers.

Not getting a form is not the same as not owing tax

Sales income is generally taxable whether or not you receive a 1099 K. If you are self employed you report it on Schedule C. A form only tells the IRS what the platform paid you, and its total can differ from your own because of fees, refunds and timing, so keep your own records.

Your state may differ

Some states set their own, lower reporting thresholds for these forms. Check your state's rules, or ask a tax professional.

What to keep

Record each order, the fees, refunds and your costs through the year. The sales and expense tracker and the tax prep workbook are built for this, and the self employment tax guide explains the next step. If your form total does not match your own records, a reconciliation of fees and refunds usually explains the gap.
