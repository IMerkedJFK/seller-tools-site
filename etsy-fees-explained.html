Etsy fees explained: what every sale really costs

Checked October 2026 for US sellers. Confirm current rates at etsy.com/legal/fees before relying on any number here.

The fees, one by one

FeeAmountWhen it applies
Listing fee$0.20When you publish, and again each time the item sells or renews
Transaction fee6.5%Item price plus any shipping you charge
Payment processing3% + $0.25The total the buyer pays (US sellers)
Offsite Ads15% (12% above $10,000 a year)Only on orders that come from an ad Etsy ran for you
Currency conversion2.5%When you list in a currency other than your payment account currency

The flat $0.45 (listing fee plus the $0.25 processing charge) hits cheap items hardest. If the same $25 item sells through an Offsite Ad, fees rise by $3.75 to $6.58, about 26 percent.
